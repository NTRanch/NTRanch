## Welcome to the Ranch

Nice Thoughts Ranch is an indie game studio building story-driven games about desire, attention, and the choice to be seen. The cowgirl in the hat is **Nancy T. Rae**, our mascot and host — folks in the know just call her Neto-chan.

## Now in development — STROBE

**STROBE** is our first title: a character-driven game following ten heroines, each with a hidden hunger and a story told in stages — from unaware, to first spark, to choosing for herself. Agency isn't a theme we gesture at; it's the structure. Every arc turns on the moment she decides.

- Design bible (the cast book): [NTRanch/Strobe](https://github.com/NTRanch/Strobe)
- Storefront: [ntranch.itch.io](https://ntranch.itch.io)

*STROBE is an adults-only (18+) title.*

## Now publishing — The Surrender Curriculum

A ten-lesson binaural hypnosis audio series: a guided voice over stepped binaural tones, lesson by lesson, each one installing the next. Headphones required. Every lesson is free, every lesson page states plainly what it installs — including its disclosed subliminal layer — and every lesson ships with a Lite edition without one.

- Start here: [Lesson One: Permission](https://ntranch.itch.io/surrender-curriculum-lesson-01-permission)
- The whole ladder: [The Surrender Curriculum collection](https://itch.io/c/8464993/the-surrender-curriculum)

*The Surrender Curriculum is adults-only (18+) listening.*

## The workshop

The Ranch builds its own tools and studies in the open:

- **Night School** — our study programme: five-pass research plans distilling published adult-animation and art tutorials into clean-room, gated production technique. [NTRanch/night-school](https://github.com/NTRanch/night-school)
- **Torogao** — our character-animation editor: 2D puppet rigs, scenes, and a playroom. [NTRanch/Torogao](https://github.com/NTRanch/Torogao)
- The jam wall — fourteen small games from the Torogao Jam Program, each an experiment that feeds the big builds.
